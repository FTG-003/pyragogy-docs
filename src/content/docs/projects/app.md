# From Reading to Relating

The Pyragogy App emerges from a simple, yet radical question:

**What if books could talk back?**


![PeeragogyBot Hero](/images/libreria.webp)

Rooted in the values of peeragogy, universal design for learning, and co-creative inquiry, this app is not just a chatbot. It is a living interface designed to transform how we relate to knowledge — shifting from static consumption to dynamic dialogue.

We believe that learning happens best when it is:
- Dialogical rather than didactic
- Open rather than prescriptive
- Co-created rather than delivered

The app embodies these principles by enabling multi-voice interaction with texts, inviting learners to reflect, question, and discover together — with the support of AI agents tuned to different perspectives.

## 🔧 Functionality: A Living Knowledge Interface

At its core, the Pyragogy App is:
- A **RAG-powered chatbot** with access to selected foundational texts.

>🧪 Currently, the app is in active testing with ***The Peeragogy Handbook*** as its foundational dataset. More sources will follow as the platform evolves.

- An interface offering **diversified AI personas**, including:
  - 🎓 *Academic*: evidence-based and methodical
  - 💡 *Explainer*: accessible and engaging
  - 🧠 *Critical*: analytical and challenging
  - 🤔 *Socratic*: reflective and inquiry-driven

The system supports:
- Real-time interaction with contextually enriched responses
- Multiple LLM backends (experimental)
- The ability to **choose AI providers and test different models**
- Integration of your own **API keys** for more powerful performance

> ⚠️ **Note**: The app is still in **prototype phase**, though already fully functional. Some **free models** may occasionally be offline — simply try another from the list.

We highly recommend creating a free account with [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/), which allows **unlimited access to free models** directly through the chatbot.

## 🚀 What’s Emerging: A Platform for Co-Learning

We envision this tool as a **co-learning companion** — not a replacement for teachers or peers, but a catalyst for reflection and exploration.

Upcoming directions include:
- Quadriphonic dialogues (multi-agent perspectives on the same topic)
- Visual annotation layers and reflective journaling
- Integration with collective knowledge-building workflows

Although we're still in the testing phase, our goal is to complete the app and make it publicly available.

### 🔑 Members of Pyragogy.org will receive **automatic access** to all premium resources.

And because we love to imagine and challenge the future, we're also **exploring tokenization of contributions**. 

Not only do we aim to reach a break-even point — we plan to **redistribute any profits** with active community members by tokenizing the value of contributions.

Frankly, we're a bit tired of the [90:9:1 rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1%25_rule).
Starting September, our **1% contributors** — those who create contents, fuel the group, and move the vision forward — will be **rewarded and compensated**.

The remaining 99%? We'll see what happens. 😉
