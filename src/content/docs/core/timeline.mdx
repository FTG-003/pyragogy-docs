## 2023 – Seeds of Transformation

1. **📜 Peeragogy Legacy Assessment (2023-09)**  
   Comprehensive review of the original Peeragogy project's impact and limitations in modern learning contexts.  

2. **🤖 AI Integration Vision (2023-11)**  
   First conceptualization of combining peer learning principles with artificial intelligence capabilities.  
   
---

## 2024 – Ideation and Foundations

1. **🌱 The Pyragogy Concept (2024-01)**  
   Official pivot from Peeragogy to Pyragogy, emphasizing the fusion of peer learning with AI-driven methodologies.  

2. **🧠 [Theoretical Framework](core/principi) Development (2024-02)**  
   Establishing core principles for AI-enhanced peer learning, including multi-agent orchestration and adaptive learning paths.  
  
3. **🧪 First Technical Prototypes (2024-03)**  
   Initial experiments with AI agents facilitating peer learning interactions and content generation.  

4. **🧩 Multi-Agent Architecture Design (2024-04)**  
   Blueprint for orchestrating multiple AI agents in collaborative learning environments.  

5. **🤖 PeeragogyBot Conceptualization (2024-05)**  
   Planning the flagship AI assistant designed to embody Pyragogy principles in practice.  
  
---

## 2024 – Development and Implementation

1. **🔧 [PeeragogyBot](projects/peeragogy) Alpha Development (2024-07)**  
   Core development of the AI assistant with basic peer learning facilitation capabilities.  


2. **🧪 Discord Integration Testing (2024-08)**  
   Implementing and testing bot functionality within Discord server environments.  

3. **🚀 First Beta Community Launch (2024-09)**  
   Launching the first Pyragogy community with active AI-assisted peer learning sessions.  

4. **🔁 Feedback Loop Implementation (2024-10)**  
   Establishing systems for continuous improvement based on community interactions and learning outcomes.
