## Scope and purpose

This page documents **verifiable occurrences of the term “Pyragogy”** in academic, scholarly, and operational sources.

It does not aim to:
- promote adoption
- infer conceptual influence
- evaluate impact

Its sole function is **epistemic traceability**.

---

## Selection criteria

An entry appears on this page **only if**:

- the term *Pyragogy* (or its direct linguistic variants, e.g. «пирагогика») is explicitly present in the source text
- the source can be independently accessed and verified
- the context of usage can be clearly identified

Sources are grouped by **origin**, not by perceived importance.

## Independent academic citations (secondary sources)

These publications are authored independently from the Pyragogy project and reference the term explicitly.

### From Classical Pedagogy to Education 4.0: Continuity of Methodology, Approaches and Principles (2022)

- **Author:** Ainur Mynbayeva  
- **Publication:** *Journal of Educational Sciences, Al-Farabi Kazakh National University*  
- **Language:** Russian (term used: «пирагогика»)  
- **URL:** https://bulletin-pedagogic-sc.kaznu.kz/index.php/1-ped/article/view/609  

> Discusses *pyragogy* («пирагогика») as part of the Education 4.0 paradigm, distinguishing it from pedagogy, andragogy, and heutagogy, and analysing learner responses to pyragogical principles.

---

### Exploring heutagogy, knowledge management, and rhizomatic learning approaches in Master's degree programs (2024)

- **Authors:** A. Mynbayeva, K. Yessenova, A. Karabutova  
- **Publication:** *Scientific Herald of Uzhhorod University. Series “Physics”*  
- **URL:** https://physics.uz.ua/web/uploads/pdf/2919-2928_Mynbayeva%20et%20al.pdf  

> References *pyragogy* as an extension of heutagogy within Education 4.0, positioning it as a theoretical basis for distance learning and postgraduate self-education.

### Perspectives of using VR to increase accessibility in distance education (2024)

- **Authors:** V. Zinchenko et al.  
- **Publication:** *Revista Eduweb*  
- **URL:** https://www.researchgate.net/publication/381846550_Perspectives_of_using_vr_to_increase_accessibility_in_distance-education
