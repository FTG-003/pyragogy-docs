This study introduces the paradigm of Cognitive Intraspecific Selection, systematically transposing biological concepts into educational contexts. The research proposes Pyragogy as an evolutionary peer-to-peer pedagogy that transforms competitive educational environments into collaborative spaces through cognitive reciprocation, ritualization of conflict, and AI facilitation. The model includes innovative metrics like the Epistemic Quality Index (EQI) and demonstrates how group cognitive fitness emerges from the dynamic interplay of ideas rather than individual competition.
